# Life Sim v0.5

Browser-based text life simulator using plain HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript.

## v0.5 additions
- Character creation: name, gender, birthplace, background.
- Elementary, middle, and high school progression with GPA and clubs/sports.
- Persistent skills: Technology, Mechanical, Business, Communication, Creativity.
- Deeper career ladders with skill requirements, promotions, raises, firing, layoffs, and quitting.
- Expanded living expenses, savings, loans, debt interest, investments, credit, and bankruptcy.
- Relationships, marriage, divorce/breakups, children, friends, and family bonds.
- Business ownership and upgrades.
- Expanded random life events.
- Aging, health decline, death, and lifetime summary.
- Achievements and milestones.
- Mobile-friendly interface.
- Autosave with localStorage plus export/import.
